## Apple Chilli

**Type:** Slow cooker, vegetarian
**Serves:** ~6

### Ingredients

- 1 large red onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 large red apple, diced
- 15 oz (270g) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 15 oz (260g) pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 15 oz (425g) fire roasted tomatoes
- 2 cups (480g) vegetable stock
- 1 cup (240g) apple juice
- ½ cup (100g) dried brown or green lentils
- 1 × 6oz (170g) can tomato paste
- 2 chipotles in adobo + 1 tbsp adobo sauce, chopped
- ½ tbsp chili powder
- ½ tbsp smoked paprika (or regular)
- ½ tbsp cumin
- ½ tbsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper

### Instructions

1. Add all ingredients into a slow cooker and stir well.
2. Place lid on slow cooker.
3. Cook on **high for 3 hours** or **low for 6 hours**.

## Seychelles Smoked Fish Salad

**Type:** No cook, salad
**Serves:** ~2

### Ingredients

- 185g smoked fish, cut into 1cm chunks
- 1 small red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 vine tomato, diced
- 1 green sour mango, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 30g lettuce (your choice)
- 1 lime
- Sugar, salt, pepper, oil

### Instructions

1. Add onion to a large bowl, season with salt and squeeze over the lime. Toss and set aside to pickle.
2. Once pickled, toss the smoked fish through and marinate for a couple of minutes.
3. Add the remaining ingredients and season with salt and pepper.
4. Serve immediately with a drizzle of oil.

## Chicken and Lentil Soup

**Type:** Stovetop
**Serves:** ~6

### Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1½ cups red lentils, rinsed
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- Salt & black pepper
- 1 cup mixed herbs (parsley, dill, mint)
- 1 cup plain full-fat yogurt (regular or Greek)
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

### Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large pot and cook onions until deeply caramelized, then add garlic and soften.
2. Add chicken, lentils, turmeric, salt, and plenty of pepper, tossing to coat.
3. Pour in 8 cups of water, bring to a boil, then simmer until lentils break down and chicken is cooked.
4. Shred the chicken, return to the pot, and stir in the herbs. Season to taste.
5. Mix yogurt with lemon juice and a pinch of salt (thin with water if using Greek yogurt).
6. Serve topped with a spoonful of the lemony yogurt.

## Shredded Tofu with Gochujang Sauce

**Type:** Air fryer / oven, vegan
**Serves:** ~2

### Ingredients

**Tofu**
- 17.5 oz extra firm tofu or tokwa

**Sauce**
- ½ cup grated Asian pear or Fuji apple, peeled
- 1–2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1½ tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1½ tsp finely grated ginger
- 3 cloves garlic, minced

**To serve**
- Chopped green onions
- Sesame seeds
- Steamed short grain or Japanese sushi rice

### Instructions

**Shredded tofu**
1. Drain tofu and remove excess water. Grate into shreds using a cheese grater.
2. Preheat air fryer or oven to 350°F / 180°C.
3. Spread shredded tofu as thin as possible on a lined baking tray or air fryer basket.
4. **Air fry:** 12–15 minutes until lightly golden. Don't overcook — it dries out quickly. Flip halfway if desired (uneven browning is fine).
5. **Oven:** 25–30 minutes.

**Sauce**
1. Mix all sauce ingredients together. Adjust to taste. Double the sauce for extra sauciness.

**Finish**
1. Transfer cooked tofu to the sauce and mix well to coat.
2. In a wok or pan over medium heat, cook down for 7–8 minutes until sauce reduces and absorbs into the tofu.
3. Taste and season further if needed.
4. Serve hot over rice, garnished with green onions and sesame seeds.
